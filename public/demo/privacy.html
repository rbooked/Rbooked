Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Rbooked (rbooked.com) collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Diners who book: your name, email, phone number, party size, date and time, and any note you add (for example an allergy or occasion).
  • Restaurants: the owner's name, login email and password (stored scrambled, never readable), and the restaurant's details, menu, photos, tables and hours.
  • Hearts: when you heart a dish or photo we save a random code in a cookie so we can remember your heart. It isn't linked to your name.
  • Basic technical data: like your internet address and browser, used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To make your booking and share it with the restaurant you booked.
  • To send emails about your booking: confirmation, changes, a reminder, and cancellation.
  • For restaurants: to run your account, send account emails (like password resets) and support you.
  • To keep Rbooked safe, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We don't sell your information, and we don't send you advertising.

Who sees it

  • The restaurant you book sees your booking details so it can seat you. It may keep them in its guest list for future visits.
  • Service providers that help us run Rbooked: Cloudflare (hosting), Resend (sending emails) and, once billing starts, Stripe (card payments for restaurants). They only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • If the law requires it, we may share information with authorities.

Cookies

We use a few small cookies: one to keep restaurant owners logged in, and one to remember a diner's hearts. We don't use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records while the restaurant's account is open, so the restaurant can see its history. Restaurants can ask us to delete their account and data. Diners can ask us to delete their details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing rbookedsupport@gmail.com. If you live in a state with its own privacy rights (such as California), we will honor those requests as the law requires.

Children

Rbooked isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Security

We protect your information with secure connections (https), scrambled passwords, and access only for the restaurant you booked and our support team. No system is perfect, so please tell us right away if you notice a problem.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell restaurants by email about important changes.

Contact

Email rbookedsupport@gmail.com.
